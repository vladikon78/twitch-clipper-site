Privacy Policy

Twitch Clipper · last updated 26 October 2026

What the app is

Twitch Clipper is a desktop application that runs only on the user's own Windows computer. It has no server and no accounts of its own. The developer does not receive, collect or store any data from users.

Data stored on your computer

  • Clips you create, their titles and settings — in the app's folder on your computer.
  • If you connect TikTok: the access token, refresh token and your TikTok display name, stored locally in the app's settings file so the app can upload clips you choose. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok.
  • If you connect a Telegram bot: its token and chat id, stored locally in the same way.

Data sent to TikTok

Only when you press “Upload to TikTok” for a clip, the app sends that video file to TikTok's Content Posting API so it appears as a draft in your TikTok inbox. The app requests only the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. It does not read your videos, followers, messages or any other data.

Removing access

Press “Disconnect TikTok” in the app's settings — the app revokes its token and deletes it from your computer. You can also remove the app's access in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
