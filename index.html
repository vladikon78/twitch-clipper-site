Twitch Clipper

A personal desktop app for Windows that finds the funniest moments of a Twitch stream by watching how the chat reacts, cuts them into short clips and lets the creator review them.

How TikTok is used

  • The creator connects their own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Nothing is posted automatically. A clip is sent to TikTok only when the creator presses “Upload to TikTok” for that specific clip.
  • The clip goes to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, upload). The creator opens it in the TikTok app, edits it and publishes it themselves.
  • The app runs entirely on the creator's computer. There is no server and no data is collected.

Коротко по-русски

Программа для Windows: находит смешные моменты стрима по реакции чата и режет из них клипы. В TikTok клип уходит только по кнопке, в черновики — публикует автор сам в приложении TikTok. Программа работает только на компьютере пользователя и никаких данных не собирает.
